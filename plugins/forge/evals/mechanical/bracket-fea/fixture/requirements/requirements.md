# Requirements -- shelf bracket

## REQ-MECH-020

While a 200 N downward load acts at the arm tip, the bracket shall have a safety factor of at least 3 against yield.

Rationale: brief.
Verify: analysis

## REQ-MECH-021

While a 200 N downward load acts at the arm tip, the arm tip shall deflect at most 0.5 mm.

Rationale: brief.
Verify: analysis

## REQ-MECH-022

The vertical leg shall have two clearance holes for M5 bolts.

Rationale: wall mounting.
Verify: inspection
