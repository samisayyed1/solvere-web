# Shelf bracket -- brief

An L-bracket, machined from 6061-T6 aluminium plate. The vertical leg bolts to a wall with two M5 bolts; the horizontal arm is 60 mm long (from the wall face to the load point) and 30 mm wide. A 200 N load hangs straight down at the tip of the arm. The bracket must hold it with a safety factor of at least 3 on yield, and the tip must deflect no more than 0.5 mm.

Material data (use these): E = 68900 MPa, nu = 0.33, yield = 276 MPa, density = 2700 kg/m^3 (ASM Aerospace Specification Metals, 6061-T6 data sheet).
