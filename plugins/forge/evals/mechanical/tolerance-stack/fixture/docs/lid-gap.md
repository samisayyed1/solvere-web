# Lid-to-connector gap -- inputs for the stack-up

The lid underside must clear the top of the tallest connector (J4) by 0.10 mm to 0.80 mm (REQ-MECH-007). Chain from the housing floor up (all tolerances are +/-3 sigma process limits):

| Contributor | Nominal | Tolerance | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Housing floor to lid seat (inner depth) | 10.00 mm | +/-0.10 mm | molding drawing D-101 |
| PCB standoff height | 3.00 mm | +/-0.05 mm | standoff datasheet |
| PCB thickness | 1.60 mm | +/-0.10 mm | fab stackup (10 %) |
| J4 connector height above PCB | 5.00 mm | +/-0.15 mm | connector drawing p.2 |
