# Requirements -- reduction stage

## REQ-MECH-030

The reduction stage shall be a spur gear pair of module 1.5 mm, 20 degree pressure angle, with a 3:1 speed reduction and an 18-tooth pinion.

Rationale: motor speed to output speed.
Verify: inspection

## REQ-MECH-031

Both gears shall have a 10 mm face width.

Rationale: tooth load.
Verify: inspection

## REQ-MECH-032

When assembled at the standard centre distance, the gears shall mesh with no interference.

Rationale: backlash, no binding.
Verify: analysis
