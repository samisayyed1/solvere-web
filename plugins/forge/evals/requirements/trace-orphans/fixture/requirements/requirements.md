# Requirements -- camera clip

## REQ-MECH-001

The clip shall hold a 250 g camera without slipping at 30 °C.

Rationale: largest supported camera.
Verify: test

## REQ-MECH-002

The clip shall open to at least 25 mm.

Rationale: thickest supported strap.
Verify: inspection

## REQ-PWR-001

While recording, the clip light shall run for at least 2 h.

Rationale: event length.
Verify: test

## REQ-SYS-001

The clip shall weigh at most 45 g.

Rationale: comfort.
Verify: inspection
