# Requirements -- tyre gauge

## REQ-SYS-001

The gauge shall measure tyre pressure from 0 bar to 11 bar.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: test

## REQ-SYS-002

The gauge shall read pressure within 0.05 bar of a reference gauge.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: test

## REQ-MECH-001

The gauge shall fit both Presta and Schrader valves.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: demo

## REQ-PWR-001

The gauge shall complete at least 700 times a 60 s measurement on one CR2032 cell.

Rationale: two checks a day for a year.
Verify: test

## REQ-SW-001

When the gauge has had no input for 60 s, the gauge shall switch off.

Rationale: battery life.
Verify: test

## REQ-MECH-002

If the gauge is dropped from 1.5 m onto concrete, then the gauge shall still meet REQ-SYS-002.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: test

## REQ-MECH-003

The gauge shall be rated IP54.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: test

## REQ-MECH-004

The gauge shall weigh at most 60 g.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: inspection

## REQ-SYS-003

While the ambient temperature is between -10 °C and 45 °C, the gauge shall meet REQ-SYS-002.

Rationale: kickoff brief.
Verify: test
