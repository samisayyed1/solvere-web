# Requirements -- smart plant pot

## REQ-SYS-001

The pot shall measure soil moisture accurately.

Rationale: core feature.
Verify: test

## REQ-SYS-002

The app shall show a fast notification when the soil is dry.

Rationale: user value.
Verify: demo

## REQ-PWR-001

The pot shall have a long battery life.

Rationale: users hate charging.
Verify: test

## REQ-MECH-001

The pot shall be robust to watering spills.

Rationale: it lives on a windowsill.
Verify: test

## REQ-UX-001

The pot shall be easy to use and intuitive.

Rationale: consumer product.
Verify: demo

## REQ-MECH-002

The pot shall hold a 12 cm nursery pot.

Rationale: standard size.
Verify: inspection
