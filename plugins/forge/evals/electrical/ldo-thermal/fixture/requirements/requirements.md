# Requirements -- sensor node supply

## REQ-ELEC-010

While supplied from 12 V +/-10 % and loaded with 250 mA, the 3.3 V rail shall stay within 3.3 V +/-3 %.

Rationale: sensor and radio supply.
Verify: analysis

## REQ-ELEC-011

While operating at 60 °C ambient and full load, the 3.3 V regulator junction temperature shall stay below 125 °C.

Rationale: regulator absolute maximum rating.
Verify: analysis
