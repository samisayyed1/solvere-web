# Mechanical notes, rev B

- Tether: 3.0 mm OD 4-core cable to the finger-clip sensor. It passes through the 3.2 mm exit hole in the +X end wall and its cores are soldered straight to PCB pads P1-P4. No boot, knot, clamp or potting: rev A's overmolded boot was dropped to save cost.
- Lid: SLA, 1.6 mm body; the optical window frame is 0.35 mm wide to maximise the aperture.
- Battery: pocket 41 x 26 x 6.0 mm, cell sits on the pocket floor; the PCB rides on four 6.0 mm bosses above it.
