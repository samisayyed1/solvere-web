# Assembly notes, rev A

1. Drop the PCB into the base slot onto the four 4.0 mm standoffs.
2. Feed the DC cable through the 5.0 mm hole in the -X wall and clamp its cores in the J3 screw terminal. Nothing else holds the cable.
3. Press the four M3 heat-set inserts into the base corners, then fit the lid (0.8 mm plate) with four M3 x 6 screws.
