Overview
The MR60FDA2 60GHz mmWave sensor detects falls within a 3x3x3 meter range and static
human presence up to 6 meters, using pre-set algorithms. It features a Seeed Studio XIAO
ESP32C6 pre-ﬂashed with ESPHome, enabling no-code Home Assistant integration, along with
a BH1750 ambient light sensor, WS2812 RGB LED, and Grove connector for expansion.

Feature 1:
Advanced mmWave Sensing for Fall and Human Presence Detection
The MR60FDA2 60GHz mmWave Sensor Module specializes in fall detection with pre-set algorithms and includes
human static presence functionality.
Human Static Presence: Effective up to 6 meters.
Fall Detection:
Detects within a 3x3x3 meter range, with a horizontal Field of View (FoV) of 120° and a vertical FoV of
100°.
*The above parameters have been tested in the empty deployment environments.
Feature 2: Powered by ESP32 & ESPHome & No-Code Integration with Home Assistant
The kit features a
Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32C6
with pre-ﬂashed
ESPHome
ﬁrmware, enabling seamless plug-and-play
integration with
Home Assistant
. Conﬁgure the device in ESPHome, and no-code setup for Home Assistant in 1-2
minutes. Thanks to the integration, you can customize detection zones and access analytics to pinpoint target
locations efﬁciently from Home Assistant.
Feature 3: Ambient Light Sensing for R
esponsive Automation
The mmWave sensor module includes a BH1750 light sensor, offering precise ambient light detection from 1 to 65,535
lux, with adjustable measurements up to 100,000 lux. Supported by
ESPHome
and
Arduino IDE
, it allows you to collect
ambient light readings as frequently as every second, enhancing your automation's responsiveness to environmental
changes.
Feature 4: Customizable Interaction
This 60GHz mmWave Sensor Module is equipped with a WS2812 RGB LED, allowing for DIY customization of the
indicator light.
Feature 5: Extended Sensing or Automation with Grove
We’ve added one additional Grove Connector to the mmWave Module for easy expansion with your own Grove sensors
and actuators (e.g., PIR and Relay).
Choose from over 300 compatible Grove Modules!
