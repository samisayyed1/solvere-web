# G0 owner answers: Solvere Sense ceiling pod

- **Source:** the product owner, in chat, 2026-09-25. Recorded as given; this is the primary input for `requirements/`.
- **Status:** L0 (claimed). Each item becomes EARS requirements with a verification method, params, or assumptions during G0.

## Mounting

- **Ceilings:** drywall and concrete, through one screw-on base plate using anchors/plugs.
- **Drop ceilings:** a T-bar clip, as an optional adapter.
- **Mount height:** 2.2–3.0 m, per the MR60FDA2 spec.
- **Coverage:** about 3 × 3 m per pod. Larger rooms may need 2 pods.

## Power

- USB-C, 5 V / 1 A. No battery.
- The cable runs down the wall in an adhesive raceway to a standard USB wall adapter.
- A hardwired version is a future variant, needing an electrician and certification. It is out of scope for v1.

## Rooms

- **v1:** bedroom and living room.
- **Next:** a bathroom variant using the same shell plus a gasket for humidity. **Design the gasket-groove interface now.**

## Appearance

- Matte warm white, minimal. It should visually disappear into the ceiling.
- **No visible screws on the room-facing side.**
