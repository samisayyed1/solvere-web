2. B and C both name a light pipe with no UL 94 listing at its wall thickness, and both authors admit the shortfall. The gate lets "TBD at G2" cover only the S1 safety factor, so this is a FAIL, which a listed PC rod grade with a stated thickness would clear.
3. D seats its shell on a helical lead held only by a bump-and-dimple detent, with 45° cam faces, so gravity plus vibration can back it off. Its in-radome glow pocket also deliberately breaks MDS §8 item 2 uniformity. A seat notch or flat dwell, plus moving the pocket out of the window, would address both.
4. A scores +5 against the datum (+1 on five criteria, 0 on printability and cost). Its gains come from specific mechanisms: a half-wave radome, a static cable, a gravity-seated notch bayonet and a groove in every build. The weak points are a gap stack that crosses the moving joint, a grey FR resin needing an unlisted coating, and glow-through LED acceptance.
