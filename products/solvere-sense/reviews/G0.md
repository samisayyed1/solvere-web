## Open questions for the owner

Each question has a default that Forge will use if it is unanswered. **[BLOCKS G1]** marks the seven questions that block G1 concept work. Source short names are in `docs/sources/README.md`.

1. **Q-01 Compute and radio [BLOCKS G1].** The MR60FDA2 kit includes a Seeed XIAO ESP32C6 (Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE, IEEE 802.15.4). Is that the pod's only compute and radio, or will the pod carry any other board (for example a voice board with microphone or speaker, another microcontroller, or a custom board replacing the XIAO)? Options: (a) kit as-is, XIAO only; (b) kit plus another board (please name it); (c) a custom board replacing the XIAO or the whole kit. *Default: (a) (A-006).*
2. **Q-02 Connectivity.** Which radios are switched on, and where does the data go? Options: (a) Wi-Fi to a local Home Assistant only, as the kit ships (ESPHome); (b) also Bluetooth; (c) Thread or Matter; (d) a cloud service or phone app. *Default: (a) (A-014).*
3. **Q-03 Target markets.** In which markets will v1 be sold first? Options: US, EU, UK, Canada, other (please name). *Default: map US, EU, UK and Canada; sell in none until a qualified human confirms the compliance route (A-013).*
4. **Q-04 Colour reference.** What defines "matte warm white"? Options: a RAL or NCS code, a named paint or filament product, or a physical sample you send. *Default: no match target; the G3 PETG prints use a stock white and colour is matched at the demo stage (A-015).*
5. **Q-05 Ceiling fixings [BLOCKS G1].** How should the base plate be fixed? Options: (a) Forge proposes screw and anchor type, size and count at G1 from the anchor makers' datasheets, for your approval; (b) you name the screw and anchor products. Are screws and anchors supplied in the box (yes or no)? *Default: (a), fixings not in the box; the instructions name them.*
6. **Q-06 Tool-free removal [BLOCKS G1].** Must an occupant be able to take the shell off the base plate by hand? Options: (a) yes, by hand (for example a twist-off); (b) no, a tool is needed (tamper-resistant); (c) the shell is never removed after installation. *Default: (a), still meeting the pull test of REQ-MECH-005.*
7. **Q-07 T-bar grid widths [BLOCKS G1].** Which drop-ceiling grid profile widths, in mm, must the T-bar clip fit? Give the widths, or name the grid systems or markets. *Default: the clip width stays an open parameter and no clip design is frozen.*
8. **Q-08 Bathroom gasket and ingress target [BLOCKS G1].** For the later bathroom variant: (1) what ingress target (an IP code, or a description such as "steam and condensation, no direct spray")? (2) any gasket preference: O-ring cord, moulded profile or foam? *Default: groove dimensions stay open parameters; G1 concepts must show a continuous groove and a fastening able to carry a gasket load.*
9. **Q-09 Temperature range.** What room temperature range must v1 work in? *Default: 0 °C to 35 °C (A-003).*
10. **Q-10 Volume and BOM scope [BLOCKS G1].** For the US$30 production BOM: (1) at what volume (units per batch or per year)? (2) does the US$30 include the USB cable, wall adapter, raceway, fixings and packaging? Note: the sensor kit alone is US$28.99 at 10+ units (Seeed product page, 2026-09-25). *Default: Seeed's 10+ tier, pod only (A-016), with the target flagged as not reachable with the kit as bought (R-002).*
11. **Q-11 Coverage versus corner gap.** Seeed publishes a 2 m maximum sensing radius, and the corners of a 3 m x 3 m square are 2.12 m from the centre. What coverage do we claim? Options: (a) keep 3 m x 3 m and let the corner tests decide; (b) a circle of 2 m radius; (c) the largest square inside that circle, 2.8 m x 2.8 m. *Default: (a) (A-011), falling back to (b) if the corner tests fail.*
12. **Q-12 Multi-pod policy.** For rooms larger than one pod covers, may two pods be used in one room? Seeed warns against radars "installed too close together" but gives no distance. Options: (a) one pod per room in v1; (b) two pods allowed, at a spacing set by our tests and Seeed's answer. *Default: (a) (A-010).*
13. **Q-13 In the box and cable route.** Does the pod ship with the USB cable, the wall adapter and the adhesive raceway? If yes, which raceway product or size, and what cable length? *Default: none in the box; the instructions name compatible parts (A-009).*
14. **Q-14 LED, light sensor and reset button [BLOCKS G1].** The kit has an RGB LED, an ambient light sensor and a reset button. Should (1) the LED show through the shell, (2) the light sensor see the room, (3) the reset button be reachable without removing the shell? *Default: (1) no, (2) no, (3) no, so the shell has no window and the pod disappears into the ceiling.*
15. **Q-15 Intended use and claims.** How will the pod be described to buyers? Options: (a) home-automation presence and fall events, with no medical or emergency claim; (b) a fall alert for elderly or vulnerable people, which likely brings medical-device or alarm rules and needs a route chosen by a qualified human. *Default: (a) (A-012).*
16. **Q-16 Occupancy.** Seeed's module specification says the radar suits bathrooms, small areas and single-person scenes. Must v1 detect falls with more than one person in the room? Options: (a) specify it for one person and say so in the instructions; (b) it must work with two or more people (needs test evidence and Seeed's confirmation). *Default: (a).*
17. **Q-17 Qualified human for safety values.** Who (name or firm) will set the retention pull-test load and hold time, the UL 94 class and the surface temperature limit, and confirm the compliance map? *Default: Forge keeps 4 x installed weight for 60 s as a placeholder (A-001), and G4 stays blocked until someone is named.*
18. **Q-18 Questions to Seeed.** May we put these questions to Seeed (the DS p.4 contact is iot@seeed.cc), and who sends them? Tilt tolerance; kit operating temperature range; whether DS p.6 power is whole-kit input power; which field of view is right; carrier-board drawing and weight; minimum spacing between pods; suitability for bedrooms, living rooms and more than one person; volume price, lifecycle and change notices; USB-C behaviour with a C-to-C source; FCC ID of the kit. *Default: Forge drafts the email and you send it.*
