# Pre-scan plan (draft)

**This is not a compliance determination.** It flags candidate standards and their sourced trigger reasons for a qualified human to review, scope and sign. Testing, certification and declarations of conformity always need an accredited lab, a notified body, or a named qualified person -- never Forge.

## ul94-flammability
- Pre-scan for: UL iQ Yellow Card lookup at actual wall thickness/colour; all testing and certification.

## ipc-2221c
- Pre-scan for: High-voltage creepage decisions, product class (1/2/3), impedance/thermal trade-offs.

## eu-rohs
- Pre-scan for: Scope determination, exemption applicability, analytical test (XRF/ICP), signing the EU DoC.

## reach-scip
- Pre-scan for: Supplier data quality, article-level calculation, ECHA submission.

## fcc-part15
- Pre-scan for: FCC-recognised accredited test lab; TCB grant for Subpart C; the SDoC US responsible party.

## ised-rss
- Pre-scan for: Accredited lab, ISED-recognised certification body, ISED registration.

## eu-red-cyber
- Pre-scan for: Risk assessment, notified-body route if a restricted clause is triggered, signing the DoC.

## cispr32-35-emc
- Pre-scan for: An accredited (ISO/IEC 17025) EMC lab and interpretation of results.

## etsi-en303645
- Pre-scan for: Third-party assessment where a national scheme requires one.

## eu-cra
- Pre-scan for: Product classification (important/critical?), conformity-assessment module or notified body, the DoC, and the actual ENISA SRP submission.

## nist-ir8259
- Pre-scan for: None strictly -- voluntary; still worth an internal checklist pass.
