# Risks

Open risks, tracked with an id so gate records and DFMEA/STPA output (`analyzing-risk`) can reference them. Likelihood and impact are qualitative (Low/Medium/High) until an analysis assigns S/O/D numbers. Source short names are defined in `docs/sources/README.md`; Q-xx are open questions in `reviews/G0.md`.

## Status values

`open` -- not yet mitigated. `mitigating` -- mitigation in progress. `closed` -- mitigated, with evidence linked. `accepted` -- a human has explicitly accepted the residual risk (record who, and when, here).
