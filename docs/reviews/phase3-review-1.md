Checked and correct:

**Verdict: FAIL.** The parts exist and the unit tests are genuinely green, but they do not work together or hold up as a whole. On a freshly scaffolded project:
- **C1:** the Stop gate blocks every Markdown or requirements edit forever.
- **C2:** anyone can satisfy the gate by writing one evidence line, or by committing the change.
- **C3:** under the Mac's default `python3` the gate and the verified-param guard switch off silently.
- **M2:** judges can write files.
- **M4:** a changed value keeps its "verified" status.

That breaks the brief's core rules that verification is the product and the maker is never the checker. To pass, Phase 3 needs:
- C1–C3 and M1–M7 fixed.
- An integration test that scaffolds the template and runs the real Stop, PostToolUse and SubagentStop hooks against it.
- The Bash residual risk written up honestly in ADR §16.
- A fresh review after that.
