# R5 — Engineering standards library (index)

- Date accessed: 2026-09-25 · Scope: current editions and one-paragraph summaries of the standards Forge links to, plus manufacturer DFM rule tables.
- Forge never reproduces standard text and never claims compliance. A qualified human or accredited body signs (credibility level L5).

The work is split across five files:

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| [R5a](R5a-standards-drawings-ipc-accessibility.md) | Drawings and tolerancing (ASME Y14.5, ISO GPS, ISO 2768/22081), electronics (IPC-2221/2222/7351, IPC-A-610, J-STD-001), accessibility and ergonomics (WCAG 2.2, ISO 9241) |
| [R5b](R5b-standards-safety-risk-se.md) | Safety (IEC 62368-1, IEC 60601-1, IEC 61508, ISO 26262, DO-178C, DO-254, UL 94), risk (AIAG-VDA FMEA, STPA), systems engineering (NASA SE Handbook, INCOSE, EARS) |
| [R5c](R5c-dfm-molding-cnc-sheetmetal.md) | DFM numeric rules: injection molding, CNC, sheet metal, each number with its source |
| [R5d](R5d-dfm-additive-snapfit-pcb.md) | DFM numeric rules: FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF, snap-fit allowable strain by material, PCB fab (JLCPCB, PCBWay), with the conflicts between sources |
| [R5e](R5e-standards-materials-radio-security.md) | Materials (RoHS, REACH), radio and EMC (FCC Part 15, ISED RSS-Gen/RSS-247, EU RED + EN 18031, CISPR 32/35), product cybersecurity (ETSI EN 303 645, NIST IR 8259, EU CRA, OWASP ASVS) |

**Provenance.** R5a–R5d are complete reports returned by sub-agents of the original R5 agent. That agent was stopped to save usage, and its sub-agents had already finished; their reports are unedited apart from a header. R5e was a separate, narrowly scoped re-run.

**Time-critical dates (2026–2027)** — see R5e for sources:
- **11 Sep 2026:** EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting obligations apply.
- **11 Dec 2026:** some RoHS lead exemptions expire.
- **11 Dec 2027:** the CRA fully applies, and RED Delegated Regulation 2022/30 is repealed in its favour.
